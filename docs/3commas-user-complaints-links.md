# 3Commas V1 shutdown / V2 migration — unhappy-user links

Collected on 11 September 2026. These are the publicly accessible pages I found where users complain about the shutdown of 3Commas V1 and the forced transition to V2.

## Direct user reviews

### Trustpilot

- [3Commas Trustpilot — all reviews](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/3commas.io)  
  Several recent one-star reviews discuss missing V1 features, manual migration, subscription uncertainty, lost configurations and refund problems.

- [Long-term customer: unclear Expert-to-Pro subscription migration](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6aa0253c9653cb5278099536) — 8 September 2026  
  The customer says support did not clearly confirm whether an Expert subscription would remain Expert after migration.

- [“Liars and thieves” — hundreds of hours needed to rebuild alerts and strategies](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6aa00bc9b945a6258127ea35) — 3 September 2026  
  Complaint about missing V1 features, 1,100 alerts/strategies needing to be rewritten and trades requiring manual management.

- [“3Commas V1 was great; the upgrade to V2 ruined it all”](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6a9f1acffd214c2aa457615f) — 7 September 2026  
  The reviewer says most of the features they used are absent while the subscription price remains the same.

- [“V2 is pure enshittification”](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6a99646cf04a057929faac85) — 3 September 2026  
  Complaint about lost historical data and bot settings, fewer features and increased pricing.

- [“Very deceptive and dishonest”](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6a930dddb5d7bfffada93215) — 29 August 2026  
  A four-year customer complains about the forced migration, differences in Grid Bot minimums, support and refund delays.

- [Support response allegedly contradicts earlier refund statements](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6a8c31fa928ae9e046fe2762) — 24 August 2026  
  Complaint about a refund refusal after activating the V2 migration code and missing DCA/backtesting features.

- [“Thieves” — user reports losses after closing positions](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6a8809dc2ee1f47330dbc067) — 21 August 2026  
  The user says bots and trades could not transfer and reports losses after closing positions. 3Commas replies that closing positions was not required.

- [“Upgrade with no rollover — you lose all your work”](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6a7e0e9058fd85f9e1c1e544) — 13 August 2026  
  Long-time user complains that trades, settings and history cannot roll over to V2.

### Google Play

- [3Commas Google Play reviews](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.threecommas.client.global&hl=en_GB)  
  Relevant reviews dated 13–14 August 2026 include a five-year customer asking for a refund because there is no rollover, and another long-time user disappointed by the new version, higher prices and changes to SmartTrade.

### YouTube

- [3Commas users need to register for V2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gNmzOrl8Dlk)  
  Migration discussion page. A negative reaction associated with this discussion complains about active deals not being exportable/importable, missing V2 features and users allegedly being banned from Discord for raising concerns. YouTube comments may require opening the page while signed in.

## Secondary page collecting complaints

- [Finestel — 3Commas Review 2026: V1 Shutdown, V2 Migration, Pricing & Features](https://finestel.com/blog/3commas-review/)  
  This article reproduces and summarizes negative Trustpilot and YouTube reactions about forced rebuilding, missing features, active positions and migration problems. Note: Finestel is also a 3Commas competitor, so the direct reviews above are stronger evidence.

## Main recurring complaints

- No automatic rollover of bots, settings, trades or historical data.
- Important V1 functions are missing or different in V2.
- Existing workflows may require many hours of manual rebuilding.
- Confusion about subscription transfers and refunds.
- Higher prices or the same price for a product some users consider less complete.
- Uncertainty about managing existing positions after the V1 shutdown.

This list includes only links I could verify publicly. Discord discussions are not included because they are not openly indexed and I did not obtain direct public message links.
